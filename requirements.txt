2. WHAT SHOULD THE SERVICE DO?

REQ-001: Create warnings
Create a warning when a valid assessment identifies one or more breached
indicators. Link it to its assessment and start with status OPEN. Allow only
one warning per assessment, following the Oracle unique constraint.
Use results supplied by the Assessment module rather than duplicating its
financial indicator calculations. Do not create warnings from missing,
invalid, or incomplete assessment data.

REQ-002: Explain Why Now
Show severity, primary driver, stress pattern, breached indicators, thresholds,
and previous-versus-current values. Provide a plain-language explanation of
why the MSME needs attention now. Store it in why_now_text. Retrieve supporting
evidence through the agreed Assessment interface, which is not yet defined.

REQ-003: Recommend actions
Provide advisory recommendations based on the stress driver:
- Revenue deterioration: discuss sales trends, key customers, and business review.
- Overdue receivables: review overdue invoices and delayed buyers; discuss
  whether eligible invoice-financing options could be explored.
- Credit utilisation: review working capital use and upcoming obligations.
- Multiple drivers: arrange RM review and consider Risk Admin escalation.
Store recommended_action. Recommendations must not execute lending or change
customer accounts.

REQ-004: View and track warnings
Show warning details, evidence, current status, decisions, interventions,
and follow-up history. Support the existing database values:
OPEN, UNDER_REVIEW, ESCALATED, INTERVENTION_ACTIVE, CLOSED_REVIEWED, CLOSED.
Restrict RMs to assigned MSMEs and representatives to their own MSME.
Risk Admin reviews high-priority and escalated cases.
Filtering, ordering, pagination, and the full transition matrix are to confirm.

REQ-005: Record RM decisions
Allow the assigned RM to accept, reject, or escalate a warning.
Accepting requires an action, owner, and follow-up date.
Rejecting requires a reason, preserves history, and uses CLOSED_REVIEWED.
Escalation sends the case for Risk Admin review.
Record decision, actor, timestamp, and reason in case history.
The schema has no dedicated decision-history table. Persistence design and
exact status mapping are to confirm.

REQ-006: Support Risk Admin decisions
Allow Risk Admin to review escalated or high-priority warnings, return cases
for further RM review, or close cases with a documented reason.
Return is specified in the image, but its target status and permitted
transitions are not defined in the DDL or contracts and are to confirm.

REQ-007: Maintain decision and activity history
Preserve creation, decisions, reasons, escalation, return, intervention changes,
follow-up reviews, and closure history. The existing activity_log can hold
actor, action, related entity, description, timestamp, and JSON metadata.
Action codes and decision metadata structure are to confirm.

REQ-008: Record interventions
For accepted warnings, record action, owner, creator, notes, active status,
and follow-up date. Multiple interventions per warning are permitted.
Use intervention.action_text, notes, created_by_user_id, is_active,
created_at, closed_at, and closed_by_user_id.
The table has no explicit owner or follow-up-date column; their persistence
requires an agreed schema or storage design.

REQ-009: Set and revise follow-up dates
Allow the RM to set and track a follow-up date. If an action cannot be completed,
record the reason, allow an alternative action, and revise the follow-up date.
Keep the case active or escalate it; do not silently close it.
Date validation, reminders, and automatic scheduling are to confirm.

REQ-010: Track monthly outcomes
When later monthly assessment data becomes available, compare relevant
indicators before and after intervention. Record reviewer, assessment, outcome,
notes, review timestamp, and whether the intervention remains active.
Enforce one review per intervention/assessment pair.
Use IMPROVING, STABILISING, STILL_STRESSED, ESCALATED.
The reference proposal describes:
- Improving/Stabilising: lower severity and improvement in at least one
  relevant indicator.
- Still Stressed: unchanged severity or a material indicator remains breached.
- Escalated: increased severity or human escalation.
The distinction between IMPROVING and STABILISING and precedence for overlapping
conditions are to confirm. Show before/after values and data dates. Do not
claim that intervention caused improvement.

REQ-011: Close cases
Allow authorised human reviewers to close cases with a documented reason,
preserving history. Record warning.closed_at. When closing an intervention,
set is_active to N and record closed_at and closed_by_user_id.
Rules for closing warnings with active interventions and the distinction
between CLOSED and CLOSED_REVIEWED are to confirm.

REQ-012: Optional notifications
The image identifies NOTIFICATION as optional. Channels, recipients, triggers,
delivery rules, and persistence are undefined. No NOTIFICATION table exists.
Confirm optional scope before implementation.

REQ-013: Persist workflow changes
Explicitly require Oracle writes for warning creation, decisions, status changes,
interventions, follow-up reviews, and activity history. These are separate write
requirements beyond the template's default fetching scope. They describe future
implementation and do not authorise database execution now.
